The Rewrite: Rooted, Radiant & Real

  • WhenSaturday, November 14, 2026 · 9 a.m. – 1:30 p.m.
  • WhereInspiredU Atlanta
  • HostsMichelle Black and Patrice Meadows

Our first event: a free, half-day workshop open to the public. Women came for breakfast and stayed to rewrite their story with an AI brand builder, a 30-second pitch builder, styling and a shop of corporate-donated clothing.

What happened at The Rewrite

Brand builderAn AI tool turned each woman's superpowers into a personal brand, leaving out the skills she'd rather not be known for.
Pitch builderGuided questions produced a 30-second pitch for a conference, a meetup, a referral and a gala.
Style sessionAI styling and a shop of corporate-donated clothing, so everyone left ready to command the room.
Food & communityA light breakfast and lunch, and a room full of women cheering each other on.
